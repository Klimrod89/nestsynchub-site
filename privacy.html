1. Introduction

Nest Sync Hub is a smart-home integration project designed to connect supported Google Nest devices with compatible home-automation platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, how that information is used, and how users can revoke access.

2. Information Accessed

Nest Sync Hub may access information made available through authorized Google Nest APIs after a user grants permission through Google OAuth.

The specific information available depends on the permissions granted by the user and the capabilities provided by Google.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the smart-home integration functionality requested by the user.

Nest Sync Hub does not use Google user data for advertising.

4. Data Sharing

Nest Sync Hub does not sell personal information.

Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested integration or where required by law.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Data storage and retention depend on the configuration of the integration and the services used by the user.

Access credentials and tokens should only be retained for as long as necessary to provide the authorized functionality.

6. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Nest Sync Hub's access to their Google account through their Google Account security settings.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authorized access credentials and related integration data.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project changes. Any updated version will be published on this page.
